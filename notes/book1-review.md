## 1. What the book is

- **Thesis:** "Islamic management" failed because it starts from Western management and Islamizes it. Book 1 starts from the Qur'an as the most upright guide (أقومية القرآن) and asks what management system emerges. It uses Western rigour (a null hypothesis, stepwise derivation, testability) to test human derivation from the text, never the text itself.
- **The toolkit:**
  - a source pyramid
  - a 7-level derivation ladder (سلم الاشتقاق), with a maqasid gate between levels 4 and 5
  - an 8-step reading protocol (بروتوكول القراءة)
  - an epistemic identity card (بطاقة الهوية المعرفية)
  - a 6-step conflict-resolution algorithm (فض التعارض)
  - the 16 pillars (الأركان)
  - QMF: 8 components, م1 to م8
  - a 7-stage action cycle: الاستشعار، التأطير الغائي، الشورى، التثبت، العزم، التوكل والتنفيذ، المتابعة والمحاسبة
  - 9 testing criteria
  - 8 hypothetical cases: B1–B4 and C1–C4

## 4. Recurring issues across the manuscript

1. **Wrong chapter numbers left in the text.**
   - Values are cited as ف9, amāna as ف7, pillar sources as "ف7-ف11". This comes from an older numbering.
   - Tool locations disagree: conflict resolution is ف6 or ف7; the reading protocol is ف3, ف5 or ف6.
   - Part boundaries disagree: Part 1 is 1–6 in one place and 4–7 in another.
2. **Premature "Book 1 is complete" endings:**
   - ch9 calls ch10 "الأخير"
   - ch10's subtitle and two "خاتمة الكتاب الأول" blocks
   - ch13 says it is the last chapter
   - ch14 has four closing boxes
3. **Counts that conflict.**
   - Pillars: 9, 13 or 16.
   - Source pyramid: 3, 5, 6 or 7 levels.
   - Card fields: 15, 16 or 18.
   - Methodological tools: 3, 4, 7 or 8.
   - Third option: 6/8 or 7/8.
   - Uncertainty levels: labels used outside the defined 5 levels, and in places the margin shrinks as the ladder rises (ch10, ch12).
   - Sunnah threshold: sahih, sahih mutawatir, or sahih/hasan.
4. **Editing residue.** "✨ توصية لجنة الأنسنة/التبسيط/الضبط الأكاديمي" labels, "الصياغة الأصلية… المعدَّلة" boxes, editor checklists left in the QA sections, "✨ إضافة لسانية — /lisan", and placeholder "مسودة أولى | معلَّق".
5. **Duplication.**
   - The 8 cases appear twice (ch13 Part 2 and ch14), with differing details.
   - The Ghoshal passage is repeated in ch2.
   - The ch1 revision box is repeated.
   - The algorithm-mapping table is repeated in ch7.
6. **Out-of-order material.** Content after the references or the bridge in ch2, 9, 10, 13 and 14. Many cited sections (§x.yب/ج) have no headings. Table numbers have gaps.
7. **Broken tables** where merged cells were lost: 2.5, 3.6, 3.9, 4.2, 13.7د, 14.6ب.
8. **Claims the text doesn't back up:**
   - "full protocol" for the new QMF components
   - "ladder applied three times" (ch8)
   - "proven in ch3" (ch4)
   - a تدبر ladder trace that doesn't exist
   - "5 schools" when only 4 are shown
   - Case 3 in ch10 said to use all 7 values
9. **Facts and attributions to check.**
   - Ghoshal was not dean of LBS.
   - Homo Sociologicus is Dahrendorf's term.
   - Tabula rasa is Locke, not Hume.
   - Cohen's κ is used with 3 raters (should be Fleiss).
   - The Shafi'i quote in ch1.
   - Baum & Locke 2004.
   - "هينسلي" should be Henderson.
   - Masorah is not rabbinic interpretation.
   - Ibn Taymiyya predates Schleiermacher by about 5 centuries, not 4.
   - Error accumulation: 6 transitions, not 7 (≈74%, not 79%).
